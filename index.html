Research & Publications

Under Review TinyML / IoMT
A Lightweight Residual 1D-CNN Architecture for Real-Time Cardiac Arrhythmia Classification on Resource-Constrained IoMT Edge Devices
Mohammad Sajedipour, Zahra Hekmati, Neda Daniaripour — Journal of Biomedical Physics and Engineering (JBPE)

Engineered an ultra-compact 1D residual network (5,621 parameters, 28.15 KB INT8 footprint) operating on single-lead ambulatory ECGs. Achieved 88.20% accuracy and 62.50% ventricular sensitivity across 27,130 unseen patient heartbeats under the strict ANSI/AAMI EC57 protocol with 45 µs execution latency.

Designing clinically interpretable Vision Transformers (ViT) integrated with Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) for multi-class neuropathological localization in cerebral magnetic resonance imaging.
